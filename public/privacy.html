HINDU ESPORTS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

HINDU ESPORTS ("the app", "we", "us") is a private clan-chat application. This page explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

What we store

  • Account information you provide: name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Content you post inside the app: chat messages, match plans, calendar events, and files/photos you upload.
  • Basic technical data needed to operate the app, such as login sessions and push-notification tokens.

Google Drive access

If the clan's owner connects a Google account, the app stores files (chat media, photos, documents) in a folder in that Google Drive account, and uses it to back up the clan's data. The app only requests access to files it creates itself in that folder — it cannot see or touch any other files in the connected Google account. Access tokens are encrypted before being stored and are never shared with any other service.

Who can see your data

Content you post is visible only to other members of your own clan inside the app. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, and we do not use your data for advertising.

Data retention

Your data is kept for as long as your account and clan exist. Clan owners can disconnect Google Drive or remove content at any time from within the app.
